Primary published silicon-behavior tables (emulator authors / community observation):
1. Graham (oxyron) — "6502/6510/8500/8502 Opcode matrix"
   https://www.oxyron.de/html/opcodes02.html
   Role: mnemonic×mode×cycle matrix; marks unstable (�) and highly-unstable (��);
   composite definitions SLO=ASL+ORA, RLA=ROL+AND, SRE=LSR+EOR, RRA=ROR+ADC,
